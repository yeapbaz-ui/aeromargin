What is data, what is model, what is simulated

  • DATA Elevation of every map cell.
  • MODEL Slope, sky view, safety classes, routes, distance, time, steps, oxygen, the point-of-no-return contour, dose rate and MERJ. These are computed by the team’s model from the elevation data.
  • SIMULATED The tank, the EVA clock, the walk, the accumulated dose, the fatigue index you set, the telemetry packet, and the science targets S1 to S5, whose positions the team picked on the grid.
  • CREW Hazard zones are entered in the app. Two are preloaded as samples.
